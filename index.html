a little about me

I studied journalism, then spent over six years in education. I taught 8th graders in government schools in Jhunjhunu, Rajasthan as a Gandhi Fellow, then joined the founding team of a nonprofit called The Unifly Collective, where I led fundraising and strategy.

Somewhere along the way, the reports, decks and campaigns I was making became the work people asked me for. Now I write and design for teams like the ones I came from, and for brands, startups and individuals too.

how i work

  1. Intro call

    We talk through your organisation, your audience and how you want the work to look and sound.

  2. Scoping the problem

    We pin down the problem you need solved and what the project has to cover.

  3. Agreeing deliverables

    We agree what I will deliver, in what format and by when.

  4. Mid-project check-ins

    We meet at set points to review progress and change course early if we need to.

  5. Delivery and revisions

    I deliver the final work. You share your feedback and I make the changes.

  6. After it ships

    I would like to hear how it works once it is in use.
